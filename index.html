The Commission

Four steps to a wardrobe of one

01

Consultation

We discuss occasion, silhouette and cloth, and take a full set of measurements — over twenty for a jacket alone.

02

Cloth & Detail

Select from the house books and specify lining, piping, buttons and monogram. The Design Studio makes it tangible.

03

Cut & Fit

An individual pattern is drafted and cut. You return for fittings as the garment is shaped to your posture.

04

Finish

Hand-set canvas, working buttonholes and your lining come together in a garment made for one person only.
