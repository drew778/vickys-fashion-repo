Our Story

Built one measurement at a time

Vicky's Fashion was founded on a simple conviction: that clothing should be made for the person, not the other way around. We keep no racks of finished suits. Instead, every commission starts blank — a conversation, a set of measurements, and a bolt of cloth chosen together.

We dress men and women alike, from a first interview suit to black-tie, tailored outerwear and the shirts worn beneath. Whatever the occasion, the promise is the same: an individual pattern, exclusive cloth, and finishing done by hand.

The Process

How a commission comes together

01

Consultation

We talk through occasion, silhouette and lifestyle, and take a complete set of measurements.

02

Cloth & Detail

Choose from the house books and specify lining, piping, buttons and monogram — try it first in the Design Studio.

03

Cut & Fit

Your pattern is drafted and the cloth cut. You return for fittings as the garment is shaped to you.

04

Finish & Care

Hand-set canvas and working buttonholes complete the piece. Your pattern stays on file for the next.
